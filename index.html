PROJECT 01

WNBA Draft Fit Model

Solo project. NCAA women's basketball stats (2022 to 2026) feed an XGBoost readiness score and a team fit score built from each franchise's statistical deficits. WNBA player seasons are clustered into six archetypes with a Gaussian Mixture Model, and the final ranking blends readiness (55%) with fit (45%).

PROJECT 02

Prediction Market Synthetics

Team project with Andrew, Drishti, Clinton, Anirudh and Yiming. The deck tests whether market makers misprice series odds conditional on single game outcomes, using synthetic positions to hedge out game exposure. Data covers 768 Kalshi contracts and second by second Polymarket order books across the 2025 and 2026 NBA and NHL playoffs. Derek led the home court pricing and series mispricing analysis.
